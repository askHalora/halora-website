The four steps

A clear path, no surprises.

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Setup form or a 20-minute call

    Fill in the setup form in about 15 minutes, or go through it with us on a call. No preparation needed.

  2. 2
    Days 2–4

    We build your Noor

    Your price list, doctors, hours, insurance list and tone of voice. We connect your WhatsApp Business number and Google Calendar. You change nothing.

  3. 3
    Days 4–6

    Your team tests it

    Message and call Noor from your own phones and tell us what to adjust. Approval mode lets you check replies before they are sent.

  4. 4
    Day 7

    Go live with a free 14-day pilot

    Noor starts answering real patients. After 14 days we look at the numbers together, and you decide.

What we need from you

Six things, one short call.

Send them by WhatsApp or email. Photos of a printed price list are fine.

Price list

Services and prices, or price ranges, that Noor may share.

Doctors & schedule

Who works when, and which services each doctor offers.

Hours & holidays

Opening hours, Fridays, Ramadan timings and public holidays.

Insurance list

Which insurers you accept. Your team confirms coverage.

WhatsApp Business number

Access to connect it to the official WhatsApp Business API.

A Google account

For the shared calendar and patient list.

After go-live

Noor keeps getting better.

Morning summary

A short report on WhatsApp every morning: bookings, after-hours replies and anything that needs your team.

Weekly tweaks

New questions Noor could not answer are added to her knowledge, with your approval.

Monthly review

We go through bookings, no-shows and reviews with you and plan the next month.

Questions

Good to know

How long until Noor is live?

Usually about a week from the first call. The build itself takes about 48 hours once we have your prices, doctors and hours.

Do I need technical skills?

No. We connect WhatsApp, the calendar and the website chat for you and train your team.

Can we start with WhatsApp only?

Yes. Many clinics start with WhatsApp and website chat, then add the phone line later.
