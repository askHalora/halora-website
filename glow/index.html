Good to know

Do I need a consultation first?

For Botox and fillers, yes. The doctor checks what suits you. For facials and laser, you can book directly.

Is laser safe for my skin type?

Our laser works on all skin types. Every new patient gets a quick patch test before the first session.

Can I cancel or move my appointment?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before. Just message Noor.

I'm pregnant or on medication. Can I book?

Please tell us before your visit so the doctor can advise which treatments are safe for you.
