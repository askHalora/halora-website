Good to know

Does Noor give medical advice?

No. Noor answers practical questions (prices, times, location, insurance) and hands anything medical to your team.

Can my team take over a chat?

Yes, at any moment, from the owner portal or WhatsApp. Noor steps back and can pick up again later.

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

We connect your WhatsApp Business number to the official WhatsApp Cloud API. We guide you through it and handle the technical part.
