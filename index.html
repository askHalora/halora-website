The problem

Patients message when your team can't answer.

Evenings, weekends, the busy hour at reception. Every unanswered message is a patient who books somewhere else.

Messages after hours

ii.

Slow replies lose bookings

People ask three clinics at once. The first clear answer with a time usually wins.

iii.

A busy front desk

The same questions about price, location and insurance, all day, while patients wait at the counter.

iv.

No idea what works

Instagram, Google or referrals? Most clinics cannot say where their patients really come from.

Questions

What clinic owners ask us.

Does Noor replace my receptionist?

No. She covers nights, weekends and busy hours and handles the repeated questions. Your team can take over any conversation at any time.

Does she really speak Arabic?

Yes. She replies in the patient's language and dialect, Gulf, Egyptian or standard Arabic, and in English, by text or voice note. Try it in the demo.

Will she give medical advice?

No. She answers only from information your clinic approves, books appointments, and passes clinical questions to your doctors.

What if she doesn't know the answer?

She tells the patient a team member will reply, alerts your staff, and logs the question so you can add the answer for next time.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Noor runs on your clinic's WhatsApp Business number. We guide you through connecting it. The phone receptionist can use your existing line or a new one.

Which calendar does it use?

Noor books into a shared Google Calendar. If your clinic uses a practice management system, tell us which one and we will check the options during the call.

What does WhatsApp charge?

Replies to patients who message you are free. Meta charges a small fee for some messages you start, such as reminders and campaigns, billed to your own WhatsApp account.

How long does setup take?

Usually 48 hours after we receive your prices, doctors and hours.
