Guardrails

What Noor will never do.

No medical advice

Noor answers practical questions only. Clinical questions go to your doctors.

No ID or card details

Noor never asks for Emirates ID, card numbers or medical history in chat.

A human when it matters

Complaints, bills and anything sensitive are handed to your staff straight away.

Emergencies first

Severe pain, bleeding or injury: the patient is told to get urgent care and your team is alerted.

Only real bookings

Noor confirms a booking only after it is in your calendar. She never invents one.

Approval mode

For the first weeks, you can approve Noor’s replies before they are sent.

Your data

Your data stays in your accounts.

Noor works on top of the accounts your clinic already owns. If you stop, everything stays with you.

  • Conversations on your own WhatsApp Business number
  • Bookings in your own Google Calendar
  • Patient lists in your own Google Sheets

Questions

Good to know

Can I switch Noor off?

Yes, at any time, for one channel or all of them. Your team can also take over any single conversation.

What if Noor does not know the answer?

She tells the patient a team member will reply, alerts your staff, and logs the question so you can add the answer for next time.

Who can see the conversations?

You decide. Owners see everything; reception can be limited to the inbox and requests.
