The service

Halora provides Noor, an AI receptionist that answers a clinic’s patients on WhatsApp, website chat and, on some plans, the phone, books appointments and sends reports. Optional add-ons include a clinic website and reminders & reviews.

Free 14-day pilot

Every plan starts with a free 14-day pilot. You do not pay anything during the pilot. If you decide not to continue, you pay nothing.

Plans, prices and payment

Plans are monthly with no long contract. Prices are in US dollars, or the same amount in AED at a fixed rate, and are shown on the pricing page. If you continue after the pilot, you pay the one-time setup fee (where it applies) and your first month. WhatsApp message fees from Meta are billed to your own WhatsApp account.

Founding clinic offer

Clinics that join by 31 December 2026 get founding prices (25% off the monthly price and the setup fee), locked for 12 months. After that, regular prices apply.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time by emailing ziad.elhoseiny@askhalora.com. Noor stops answering at the end of the month you have paid for. Your WhatsApp number, calendar and data stay yours.

Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate information about your services, prices, doctors and hours, and tell us when it changes.
  • Make sure you are allowed to message your patients, including reminders and campaigns.
  • All medical decisions stay with your clinic’s qualified staff.

What Noor does and does not do

Noor answers from the information you approve, never gives medical advice and hands sensitive or unusual conversations to your team. Like any software, Noor can make mistakes, so your team can review and take over any conversation at any time.

Demos

Demo clinics on this website, such as BrightSmile Dental, Glow Skin Clinic, Lumina Dental and Pearl Demo Dental, are fictional. Nothing booked in a demo is real.

Liability

We work hard to keep Noor reliable, but we cannot promise the service will always be uninterrupted or error-free. To the extent the law allows, our total liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the three months before the event.

Contact

Questions about these terms? Email ziad.elhoseiny@askhalora.com.
