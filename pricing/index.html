Questions

About pricing

What does WhatsApp charge?

Replies to patients who message you are free. Meta charges a small fee for some messages you start, such as reminders and campaigns, billed to your own WhatsApp account.

How long does setup take?

The setup form takes about 15 minutes. Noor is usually ready within 48 hours of receiving your prices, doctors and hours, and you can go live within about a week, once your team has tested her.

What happens after the free pilot?

If you continue, you pay the one-time setup and your first month. If Noor does not bring you bookings, you walk away and pay nothing.
