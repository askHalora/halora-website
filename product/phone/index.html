Questions

Good to know

Which plan includes calls?

Phone answering is included in Front Desk + Phone and Group. You can also add it to Starter or Front Desk as an add-on.

Can patients still reach a person?

Yes. Noor transfers calls or arranges a callback whenever a patient asks for a person.

Does Noor give medical advice?

No. Noor answers practical questions (prices, times, location, insurance) and hands anything medical to your team.
