Who we are

Halora builds Noor, an AI receptionist for clinics in Dubai and the Gulf. In this policy, “we” means Halora. You can reach us at ziad.elhoseiny@askhalora.com.

What we collect on this website

  • Forms: when you fill in the setup form, the pilot form or book a call, we collect what you type: your name, clinic name, WhatsApp number, email, city and the clinic details you share.
  • Demo chat and voice demo: messages you send to the demo Noor are processed to generate replies. The demo clinics are fictional and no real appointment is made.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider may log standard information such as your IP address and browser type for security.

How we use it

To reply to you, set up and run your pilot or subscription, book calls, improve Noor and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Patient data at clinics using Noor

When a clinic uses Noor, the clinic decides how its patient data is used, and we process it on the clinic’s behalf only to provide the service. Conversations run on the clinic’s own WhatsApp Business number, bookings go into the clinic’s own Google Calendar and patient lists stay in the clinic’s own Google Sheets.

Data & safety: Noor never asks patients for ID numbers, card details or medical history, never gives medical advice, and hands anything sensitive to the clinic’s team.

Service providers

To run the service we use trusted providers, for example: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), Google (Calendar and Sheets), our automation and hosting providers, ElevenLabs for the phone voice, and AI model providers that process messages to write Noor’s replies. They only receive what they need to do their part.

How long we keep data

We keep enquiry and form data while we are in contact with you, and for as long as you are a customer. Demo chat data is cleaned up regularly. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email ziad.elhoseiny@askhalora.com and we will reply within a reasonable time.

Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The website uses your browser’s local and session storage only for small conveniences, for example to remember that you have already seen the opening animation.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
